# Privacy notice: joining {association_name}

*This is the template notice of the platform. The association reviews it before use and may
replace it with its own.*

**Who is responsible.** {association_name} (the apartment association) decides why and how your
data are used. The board of the association is your contact, through the site {site_url} or
the contacts the association has given you.

**What we collect and why.** When you use the join code of an apartment, a parking space or a
garage, you give your full name, phone number and e-mail address. The chairman of the association
uses them only to check that you are an owner of that apartment, parking space or garage, for
example by comparing the phone number with the association's list of members or by calling you. If
the chairman confirms you, an invitation to the site is sent to your e-mail address.

**Legal basis.** The association's legitimate interest in managing its members and the
apartment building (Article 6(1)(f) of the General Data Protection Regulation), and steps you ask
for before joining (Article 6(1)(b)).

**Who sees the data.** Only the chairman of the association, until the decision. The site is run
for the association by its server operator, who processes the data only on the association's
behalf.

**How long.** Your name, phone number and e-mail address are deleted as soon as the chairman
decides, or after 30 days without a decision. If you are confirmed, the e-mail address stays in the
invitation and later in your account. A record that a request for the apartment, parking space or
garage was confirmed, rejected or expired stays, without your personal data.

**Your rights.** You may ask the association for access to your data, to correct or delete them,
or to restrict their use, and you may object to their use. You may complain to the data
protection authority (in Estonia: Andmekaitse Inspektsioon, www.aki.ee).
